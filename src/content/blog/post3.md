## Communication is the key
- Bad communication can destroy good products
- Clear thinking leads to clear communication
- Be humble, learn from everyone and take notes
- Over communication is better than under communication
## Demos > Meeting Discussions
- Demos beat daydreaming discussion on the product
- They don't have to be 100% perfect
- Helps you to be on track
## Upskilling Forever
- Learning should never stop
- Improve 1% every day
- Hard work > talent
## Fail fast, learn faster
- Ship that imperfect product rather than sitting on it
- Expect harsh feedback, iterate on it
- Let actual users guide you
- Embrace tech innovation but prioritize stability
## Empathy goes a long way
- Always assume their good intent
- Be kind yet help them with good criticism
